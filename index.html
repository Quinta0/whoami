01 · Performance · before

A game server, mid-launch, running hot.

In autumn 2026 I volunteered on the community revival of a mobile game while its player base grew from 2,500 to over 14,000.

Here are 14,000 requests, one cube each, stacked by how long they took. The orange cubes are the slowest 5%. On the test server that tail started at 747 ms.

02 · Performance · after

The same requests, eleven times faster.

p95 fell from 747 ms to 66 ms. Forty commits in five pull requests, each reviewed and merged by the project's founder.

  • +27% throughput, 210 to 266 requests per second
  • −25% CPU per request in the project benchmark
  • 30 → 1 min CI runs, with ccache, vcpkg and cargo caching
  • ~70% of the Cloudflare bill traced to one Worker; moving downloads to R2 more than halved Worker costs

Checked by diffing 400 responses and the final database between versions. Founder's reference available on request.

03 · Efficiency

85 watts of idle, cut to 17.

One of my production servers, before and after. Each layer of cubes is one watt it draws while doing nothing.

The 80% came from BIOS voltage offsets, PL1/PL2 power limits and C-state tuning, then ASPM, powertop and drive spin-down. Every change was benchmarked and stability-tested before it stayed. About CHF 160 a year, from one machine.

The same habit goes into the hardware itself: more than 25 systems built, upgraded and repaired for private clients since 2020, down to board-level repairs.

04 · Security
